## About Me

Flutter developer with 3+ years of experience building and shipping cross-platform mobile applications for iOS and Android. Skilled in Dart, Firebase, RESTful APIs, and state management (BLoC/Cubit, Provider, GetX), with a strong focus on clean architecture and production-grade code. I own full project lifecycles end to end — from architecture through App Store and Google Play deployment — across hospitality, fintech, and SaaS domains.

- 🌍 Full English/Arabic localization & RTL support across multiple production apps
